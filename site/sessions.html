Sessions of Therapy

Groggy logs: how one family caregiver used AI tools, day and night, to the point of explaining their own work back to them. Every session is a dot in the black box. We count the sessions. We do not show what they say.

The black box

Each dot is one AI session. The dot size shows how many messages it had. Dots group by topic. The moving sparks show the same week jumping from one topic to another. Type in the search box, or click a topic, to zoom in.

drag to turn · click a dot or a topic to zoom
